Terminal Island

Twenty years from now, almost every car drives itself. Inertia barriers make crashes impossible. On a half-built island at the edge of the city, the last people who still drive by hand come out at night to race.

Three districts are open: Neon Row, Dockside and Old Terminal. The rest of the island is still under construction.
